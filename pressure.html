Pressure Converter

Convert pressure quickly between metric, imperial and scientific pressure units.

Enter a value and click Convert Pressure.

How to Convert Pressure?

Enter a pressure value, select the unit you are converting from, choose the unit you want to convert to, and click Convert Pressure.

Converted Value = Value × Conversion Factor

Supported Pressure Units

This converter supports Pascal (Pa), Kilopascal (kPa), Megapascal (MPa), Gigapascal (GPa), Bar, Millibar (mbar), Atmosphere (atm), PSI, PSF, Millimeter of Mercury (mmHg), Torr and Inch of Mercury (inHg).

Example

If you convert 1 atmosphere to Pascal:

1 atm = 101325 Pa
