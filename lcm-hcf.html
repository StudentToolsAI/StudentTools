🔢 What are LCM and HCF?

LCM stands for Least Common Multiple. It is the smallest positive number that is a multiple of all the given numbers.

HCF stands for Highest Common Factor. It is the largest number that divides all the given numbers exactly.

📐 LCM Formula

LCM(a,b) = (a × b) ÷ HCF(a,b)

💡 Example

Let's take the numbers 12 and 18.

HCF(12,18) = 6
LCM(12,18) = 36

Therefore, the HCF is 6 and the LCM is 36.
