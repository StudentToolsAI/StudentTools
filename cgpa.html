CGPA Calculator

Calculate your average percentage and approximate CGPA using your subject marks.

Enter your subject marks and click Calculate CGPA.

How to Calculate CGPA?

Enter the marks obtained in each subject. You can add or remove subjects according to your needs. The calculator supports up to 10 subjects.

Average Percentage = Total Marks ÷ Number of Subjects

Approximate CGPA = Average Percentage ÷ 9.5

Example

If your average marks are 70%, your approximate CGPA will be:

70 ÷ 9.5 ≈ 7.37 CGPA
