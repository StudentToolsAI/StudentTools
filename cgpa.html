How to Calculate CGPA?

Enter the marks obtained in each subject. The calculator calculates your average percentage and then estimates the CGPA.

Percentage = Total Marks ÷ Total Maximum Marks × 100
Approximate CGPA = Percentage ÷ 9.5

Example

If your average percentage is 85%:

85 ÷ 9.5 ≈ 8.95 CGPA
