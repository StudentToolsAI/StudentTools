How to Calculate CGPA?

Enter your marks for each subject. The calculator first calculates your average percentage and then converts it into an approximate CGPA.

Percentage = Total Marks ÷ Total Maximum Marks × 100
Approximate CGPA = Percentage ÷ 9.5

Example

If your five subject marks are 90, 85, 80, 95 and 90, your average percentage is 88%.

88 ÷ 9.5 ≈ 9.26 CGPA
