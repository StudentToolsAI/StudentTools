Time Converter

Convert time quickly between seconds, minutes, hours, days and weeks.

How to Convert Time?

Enter a value, select the unit you are converting from, choose the unit you want to convert to, and click the Convert Time button.

Converted Value = Value × From Factor ÷ To Factor

Supported Time Units

This converter supports seconds, minutes, hours, days and weeks.

Common Time Conversions

1 minute = 60 seconds

1 hour = 60 minutes

1 day = 24 hours

1 week = 7 days

Example

If you convert 2 hours to minutes:

2 hours = 120 minutes
