Simplifying a Ratio

A ratio can be simplified by dividing both numbers by their greatest common divisor (GCD).

Simplified Ratio = A ÷ GCD : B ÷ GCD

Example

Suppose the ratio is: 24 : 36

The GCD of 24 and 36 is 12.

24 ÷ 12 = 2
36 ÷ 12 = 3

Therefore: 24 : 36 = 2 : 3

Equivalent Ratios

Equivalent ratios have the same relationship between their values. To create an equivalent ratio, multiply both sides by the same number.

A : B = (A × M) : (B × M)

Example

Original ratio: 3 : 5

Multiplier: 4

3 × 4 = 12
5 × 4 = 20

Therefore: 3 : 5 = 12 : 20

Finding a Missing Value

When one value in a proportion is unknown, cross multiplication can be used to find it.

A : B = C : X

X = (B × C) ÷ A

Example

Consider: 2 : 5 = 8 : X

Using the formula:
X = (5 × 8) ÷ 2
X = 40 ÷ 2
X = 20

Therefore: 2 : 5 = 8 : 20

What Is a Ratio?

A ratio is a way of comparing two quantities. Ratios are commonly written using a colon, such as 2 : 3.

For example, if a class has 10 boys and 15 girls, the ratio of boys to girls is 10 : 15.

Example:

10 : 15

Divide both numbers by 5:
10 ÷ 5 = 2
15 ÷ 5 = 3

Simplified ratio: 2 : 3
