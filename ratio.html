Frequently Asked Questions

What is a ratio?
A ratio compares two or more quantities and shows their relationship using numbers such as 2 : 3.

How do I simplify a ratio?
Enter both numbers in the Simplify Ratio calculator. The calculator divides them by their greatest common divisor and gives the simplest ratio.

What is an equivalent ratio?
Equivalent ratios have the same relationship. Multiplying both parts of a ratio by the same positive number creates an equivalent ratio.

How do I find a missing value?
Enter the known values in the proportion A : B = C : ?. The calculator uses the proportion to calculate the missing value.

Yes. The Ratio Calculator is completely free and can be used without creating an account.
