Temperature Converter

Convert temperature quickly between Celsius, Fahrenheit, Kelvin and Rankine.

Enter a temperature and click Convert Temperature.

How to Convert Temperature?

Enter a temperature, select the unit you are converting from, choose the unit you want to convert to, and click Convert Temperature.

Temperature conversions use standard mathematical conversion formulas.

Supported Temperature Units

This converter supports Celsius (°C), Fahrenheit (°F), Kelvin (K) and Rankine (°R).

Example

If you convert 0°C to Fahrenheit:

0°C = 32°F
