Attendance Calculator

Calculate your attendance percentage and find how many classes you need to attend to reach 75%.

Enter your class details and click Calculate Attendance.

How to Calculate Attendance?

Attendance percentage is calculated by dividing the number of classes attended by the total number of classes and multiplying the result by 100.

Attendance % = (Classes Attended ÷ Total Classes) × 100

Example

If you attended 36 classes out of 45 classes:

(36 ÷ 45) × 100 = 80%
You have 80% attendance.

How Many Classes Are Needed for 75%?

If your attendance is below 75%, the calculator also estimates how many consecutive classes you need to attend to reach 75% attendance.
