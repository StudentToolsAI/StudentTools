How to Use the Discount Calculator?

Enter the original price, enter the discount percentage, choose the quantity, and optionally enter a GST percentage.

Click Calculate Discount to instantly see the discount amount, final price, total savings and tax.

Discount Calculator Formula

The discount amount is calculated by multiplying the original price by the discount percentage.

Discount Amount = Original Price × Discount % ÷ 100

Final Price = Original Price − Discount Amount

Example

Original Price: ₹1,000
Discount: 20%

Discount Amount:
₹1,000 × 20 ÷ 100 = ₹200

Final Price:
₹1,000 − ₹200 = ₹800

You save: ₹200

Discount with GST

You can also use this calculator to calculate the final price after adding GST.

GST Amount = Price × GST % ÷ 100

Price with GST = Price + GST Amount

Example

Original Price: ₹2,000
Discount: 10%
GST: 18%

Discount Amount: ₹200
Price after discount: ₹1,800
GST on ₹1,800: ₹324

Final Price: ₹2,124

What Is a Discount?

A discount is a reduction from the original price of a product or service. It is usually expressed as a percentage.

For example, a 25% discount means that 25 out of every 100 parts of the original price are reduced.

Example:

Original Price = ₹800
Discount = 25%

Discount = ₹800 × 25 ÷ 100
Discount = ₹200

Final Price = ₹800 − ₹200
Final Price = ₹600
