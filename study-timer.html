How to Use the Study Timer

The Study Timer helps you divide your study time into focused sessions followed by short breaks. This can make long study periods easier to manage.

Step 1 — Choose a Mode

Select Classic, Focus, Deep Study or create your own custom timer.

Step 2 — Enter Your Subject

Enter what you are studying, such as Mathematics, Physics or a particular chapter.

Step 3 — Start Studying

Press Start and focus until the session ends.

Step 4 — Take Your Break

When a focus session finishes, use the break period to rest before starting another session.

Study Timer Modes

🍅 Classic Pomodoro

25 minutes of focus followed by a 5-minute break.

⚡ Focus Mode

50 minutes of focused study followed by a 10-minute break.

🧠 Deep Study

90 minutes of focused study followed by a 20-minute break.

⚙️ Custom Mode

Choose your own focus, short-break and long-break durations.

How the Timer Works

The progress indicator represents how much of the current session has been completed.

Progress = Completed Time ÷ Total Session Time × 100

Example Study Session

Suppose you choose the Classic 25/5 mode.

You study Mathematics for 25 minutes .

After the focus session ends, the timer moves to a 5-minute break .

After completing the session, your sessions and focus time are updated automatically.

Smart Study Tips

📱 Remove Distractions

Keep unnecessary notifications and distractions away while your focus session is running.

📚 Choose One Task

Give each session one clear study objective.

☕ Respect Breaks

Use breaks to rest instead of immediately starting another demanding task.

🎯 Track Your Progress

Set a realistic daily goal and use the progress indicator to see how much study time you have completed.
