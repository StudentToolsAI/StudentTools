How to Use the Statistics Calculator?

Enter a list of numbers separated by commas, spaces or new lines. Click Calculate Statistics to instantly find important statistical values from your data set.

Mean = Sum of all values ÷ Number of values

What Does It Calculate?

This calculator provides the count, sum, minimum, maximum, mean, median, mode, range, variance, standard deviation and quartiles Q1, Q2 and Q3.

Mean

The mean is the average of all values in the data set.

Mean = Σx ÷ n

Median

The median is the middle value after the data has been arranged from smallest to largest.

Standard Deviation

Standard deviation measures how spread out the values are from the mean.

Population SD = √Variance

Example

For the data set 10, 20, 20, 30 and 40, the calculator determines all major statistical measures automatically.
