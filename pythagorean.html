📐 Pythagorean Theorem Formula

a² + b² = c²

The Pythagorean theorem is used to find the missing side of a right-angled triangle. Here, a and b are the two shorter sides and c is the hypotenuse.

🧮 What is the Pythagorean Theorem?

The Pythagorean theorem states that the square of the hypotenuse is equal to the sum of the squares of the other two sides.

c = √(a² + b²)

💡 Example

Suppose a right triangle has Side a = 3 and Side b = 4.

c = √(3² + 4²)
c = √(9 + 16)
c = √25 = 5

Therefore, the hypotenuse is 5 units.

🎯 Quick Tips

• The hypotenuse is always the longest side.

• This theorem works only for right triangles.

• Enter positive numbers only.
