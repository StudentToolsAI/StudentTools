Pythagorean Theorem Formula

a² + b² = c²

Here a and b are the two legs of a right triangle, while c is the hypotenuse.

📚

What is the Pythagorean Theorem?

The Pythagorean theorem is a mathematical rule used to calculate the length of a missing side in a right-angled triangle.

The hypotenuse is always the longest side of the right triangle and is opposite the right angle.

💡

Example

Suppose a right triangle has:

  • Side a = 3
  • Side b = 4

To find the hypotenuse:

c = √(a² + b²)
c = √(3² + 4²)
c = √25
c = 5

Therefore, the hypotenuse is 5 units.
