📄 How to Use?

Select the side you want to calculate, then enter the two known side lengths.

💡 Example

If Side A = 3 and Side B = 4:

c = √(3² + 4²) = 5

📐 Pythagorean Theorem Formula

The Pythagorean theorem states that the square of the hypotenuse equals the sum of the squares of the other two sides.

a² + b² = c²
c = √(a² + b²)

🎯 Important Tips

• The hypotenuse is always the longest side.

• This calculator works for right triangles.

• Enter positive numbers only.

• All sides should use the same unit.
