GPA Calculator

Calculate your GPA easily using your subject grade points.

Enter your grade points and click Calculate GPA.

How to Calculate GPA?

Enter the grade point for each subject. You can add or remove subjects according to your needs. The calculator supports up to 10 subjects.

GPA = Total Grade Points ÷ Number of Subjects

Example

If your grade points are 8, 7, 9 and 8, your GPA will be:

(8 + 7 + 9 + 8) ÷ 4 = 8.00 GPA
