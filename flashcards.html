How to Use Flashcard Maker

Enter a question in the Question / Front box and enter its answer in the Answer / Back box.

Click Add Flashcard to create your study card.

Click the flashcard to flip it and reveal the answer. Use the navigation buttons to move between your cards.

Flashcard Study Tips

  • Keep questions short and clear.
  • Put one important concept on each card.
  • Try answering before flipping the card.
  • Use flashcards for definitions, formulas and important facts.
  • Shuffle your cards to avoid memorizing their order.
