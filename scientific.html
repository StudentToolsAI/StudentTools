Scientific Calculator

This free scientific calculator is designed for students and everyday mathematical calculations. It supports basic arithmetic as well as advanced mathematical functions.

Features

Use addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, percentages, powers, square roots, cube roots, factorials, logarithms, natural logarithms, exponential functions, absolute values and trigonometric functions.

sin, cos, tan • log • ln • √ • x² • xʸ • x!

Angle Modes

Trigonometric calculations can be performed in Degree (DEG), Radian (RAD) or Gradian (GRAD) mode.

Example

To calculate the square of 12, press x² after entering 12.

12² = 144
