Volume Converter

Convert volume quickly between metric, imperial and US customary units.

Enter a value and click Convert Volume.

How to Convert Volume?

Enter a value, select the unit you are converting from, choose the unit you want to convert to, and click the Convert Volume button.

Converted Value = Value × Conversion Factor

Supported Volume Units

This calculator supports milliliters, centiliters, deciliters, liters, cubic meters, cubic centimeters, cubic millimeters, cubic inches, cubic feet, cubic yards, US gallons, US quarts, US pints, US cups, US fluid ounces, Imperial gallons, Imperial quarts, Imperial pints and Imperial fluid ounces.

Example

If you convert 1 liter to milliliters:

1 L = 1000 mL
