Probability Formula

When all possible outcomes are equally likely, probability can be calculated using:

Probability = Favorable Outcomes ÷ Total Possible Outcomes

The probability of an event is always between 0 and 1, or between 0% and 100%.

Example

Suppose a box contains 10 balls and 3 of them are red.

Favorable Outcomes: 3
Total Possible Outcomes: 10

Probability:
3 ÷ 10 = 0.3

Therefore: Probability = 0.3 = 30%

What is Probability?

Probability is a measure of how likely an event is to happen. It is commonly used in mathematics, statistics, science and many real-world situations.

Probability 0

An event with probability 0 is impossible.

Probability 1

An event with probability 1 is certain to happen.

Probability Between 0 and 1

A value between 0 and 1 represents different levels of likelihood.
