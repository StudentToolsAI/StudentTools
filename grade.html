Calculate your percentage and find your corresponding grade instantly.

How to Calculate Grade?

First calculate your percentage by dividing the obtained marks by the total marks and multiplying by 100. The percentage is then matched with the corresponding grade range.

Percentage = (Obtained Marks ÷ Total Marks) × 100

Grade Scale

A+ : 90% – 100%
A : 80% – 89.99%
B : 70% – 79.99%
C : 60% – 69.99%
D : 50% – 59.99%
F : Below 50%

Example

If you score 450 marks out of 500:

(450 ÷ 500) × 100 = 90%
Grade = A+
