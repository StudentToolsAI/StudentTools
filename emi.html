EMI Calculator Formula

The standard reducing-balance EMI formula is:

EMI = P × R × (1 + R)N ÷ ((1 + R)N − 1)

P = Loan Principal
R = Monthly Interest Rate
N = Number of Monthly Payments

What Is EMI?

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Instalment. It is the regular monthly amount paid toward a loan.

A typical EMI contains both a portion of the loan principal and the interest charged on the outstanding balance.

What Affects Your EMI?

  • Loan Amount: A higher loan amount generally increases EMI.
  • Interest Rate: A higher interest rate generally increases EMI and total interest.
  • Loan Tenure: A longer tenure can reduce the monthly EMI, but usually increases total interest paid.
