Area Converter

Convert area quickly between metric, imperial and common land measurement units.

Enter a value and click Convert Area.

How to Convert Area?

Enter an area value, select the unit you are converting from, choose the unit you want to convert to, and click Convert Area.

Converted Value = Value × From Factor ÷ To Factor

Supported Area Units

This converter supports square millimeters, square centimeters, square meters, square kilometers, square inches, square feet, square yards, square miles, acres and hectares.

Common Area Conversions

1 m² = 10,000 cm²

1 km² = 1,000,000 m²

1 acre ≈ 4,046.856 m²

1 hectare = 10,000 m²

Example

If you convert 1 hectare to square meters:

1 hectare = 10,000 m²
