How to Convert Speed?

Enter a value, select the unit you are converting from, choose the unit you want to convert to, and click the Convert Speed button.

Converted Speed = Value × Conversion Factor

Supported Speed Units

This calculator supports meters per second, kilometers per hour, miles per hour, feet per second, knots, Mach, kilometers per second, meters per hour, centimeters per second, centimeters per hour, feet per hour and yards per second/hour.

Example

If you convert 100 kilometers per hour to miles per hour:

100 km/h ≈ 62.1371 mph
