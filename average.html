Average Calculator

Calculate the average of multiple numbers quickly and accurately.

Enter your numbers and click Calculate Average.

How to Calculate Average?

To calculate the average, add all the numbers together and divide the total by the number of values.

Average = Sum of all numbers ÷ Number of values

Example

Suppose the numbers are 10, 20, 30 and 40.

Average = (10 + 20 + 30 + 40) ÷ 4

Average = 25
