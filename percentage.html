How to Calculate Percentage?

Percentage is calculated by dividing the obtained marks by the total marks and multiplying the result by 100.

Percentage = (Obtained Marks ÷ Total Marks) × 100

Example

If you scored 450 marks out of 500:

(450 ÷ 500) × 100 = 90%
